How it works: everyone starts at 1000 unless a starting rating was assigned. Win chance depends on the rating gap (about 64% for the higher-rated player at a 100-point gap). After each match the winner gains 32 × (1 − win chance) points and the loser loses the same amount. Only who won counts, not the score. Players who miss a week keep their rating.
